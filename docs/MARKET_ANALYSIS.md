# EuroComply Market Analysis

## Executive Summary

The EU DPP market presents a "blue ocean" opportunity in the SME segment. While enterprise players (SAP, Siemens) fight for the top 1%, **99% of EU businesses have no affordable compliance solution**. EuroComply fills this gap with a Compliance-First Product Information Management (PIM) platform where users can review and issue Digital Product Passports when product data is complete.

---

## The Market Opportunity

### Forced Market Adoption

Unlike typical B2B software, DPP adoption is **driven by law (EU ESPR), not business choice**. Non-compliance means exclusion from the EU market.

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Legal mandate | 100% adoption required for covered products |
| Deadline | 2027 for textiles, batteries; 2029-2030 for others |
| Penalty | Market exclusion (cannot sell in EU) |

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)

The volume of potential SME customers is massive:

| Sector | EU Companies | SME % | DPP Deadline |
|--------|--------------|-------|--------------|
| **Textiles & Clothing** | 143,000 - 170,000 | 99.5% | 2027 |
| **Furniture** | ~130,000 | 95%+ | 2029 |
| **Construction Products** | 500,000+ | 90%+ | 2030 |
| **Electronics** | 100,000+ | 85%+ | 2030 |

**Key insight:** 99.5% of textile companies are SMEs, mostly micro-enterprises (<10 employees).

## Success Factors for SME Market

### 1. Template-Driven Compliance

SMEs don't know what data is required. We provide:

| Product Type | Pre-built Template | Mandatory Fields Highlighted |
|--------------|-------------------|------------------------------|
| Cotton T-Shirt | ✅ | Fiber %, origin, care instructions |
| Recycled Hoodie | ✅ | Recycled content %, certifications |
| Denim Jeans | ✅ | Water usage, chemical treatments |
| Leather Bag | ✅ | Animal welfare, tanning process |

SMEs often lack sustainability data. We help:

| Missing Data | Our Solution |
|--------------|--------------|

| Industry benchmarks | "Average cotton t-shirt: 5.5 kgCO2e" |
